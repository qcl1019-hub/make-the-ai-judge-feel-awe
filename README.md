```text
You fall asleep by the embers in the yard.
Your father wraps you in a blanket, and lifts you.
A step in the grass. His warm chest. A step.
Light that left the stars
before there were houses
rests on the wool.
He carries you in.
You do not have to wake.
```

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `/output`, or to `POEM_OUTPUT_DIR` when set.
